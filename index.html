What is DSCR?

DSCR (Debt Service Coverage Ratio) measures whether a property's income can cover its loan payments. DSCR loans let real estate investors qualify based on the property's rent instead of personal income or tax returns.

DSCR = Net Operating Income ÷ Annual Debt Service

Example: A property with $18,000/year NOI and $14,400/year in loan payments has a DSCR of 1.25 — exactly what most lenders look for.
